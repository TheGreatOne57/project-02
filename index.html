01

THE FIRST TIME I SAW YOU

You were finally standing in front of me.

I saw you coming toward me in the distance, and for a second I couldn't believe this was actually happening.

After all the calls, messages, games and late nights, you weren't behind a screen anymore. You were really there.

But your family was nearby, and you were scared of what they might think. So when I saw you coming, I had to turn around and pretend I wasn't waiting for you.

Then we finally got somewhere out of sight.

02

THE FIRST HUG

And then I finally got to hold you.

Once we were away from everyone, we finally hugged for the first time.

You were so much shorter than me that your face disappeared right into my chest.

And honestly, I was just happy to finally have you there in my arms.

After waiting so long, that hug felt real.

03

THE FIRST WALK

Just you, me, and a walk to KFC.

You were nervous because you're usually an inside girl and don't get many chances to go out like that.

So I held your hand and walked beside you while you kept smiling.

You kept rubbing up on me while we were walking, and I remember feeling so happy just being there with you.

Somehow, a walk to KFC became one of my favorite memories.
